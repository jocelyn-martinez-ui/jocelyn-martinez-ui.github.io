# Holland Walking Tour — mobile experience redesign

This mockup suite reframes Holland Walking Tour as **Holland Walks**: an accessible, editorial self-guided tourism experience for Holland, Michigan.

## Redesigned journey

1. **Discover** — shows useful tours, time, distance, difficulty, accessibility, and nearby options immediately.
2. **Tour details** — helps visitors evaluate a route before committing and supports offline download.
3. **Build your walk** — combines search, filters, explicit selection states, and a live time/distance estimate.
4. **Route overview** — confirms stop order, route shape, amenities, difficulty, and offline status.
5. **Live navigation** — prioritizes one large, glanceable direction with route progress and destination context.
6. **Stop story** — balances photography, audio, transcript access, short-form history, and a visible next step.
7. **Tour tools** — supports pauses, step-free detours, restrooms, benches, weather, skipping, and ending safely.
8. **Tour complete** — summarizes the walk and offers optional saving, sharing, reflection, and nearby discovery.

## Key UX changes

- Removed the blocking introductory carousel and delayed location access until it has clear contextual value.
- Merged “Choose a Tour” and “Choose a Tour Type” into a useful Explore experience instead of two abstract decision screens.
- Replaced ambiguous **Next** and **Create** labels with action-specific copy.
- Added duration, distance, difficulty, stop count, accessibility, and offline availability before a tour begins.
- Made custom selection states explicit with labels and checkmarks rather than color alone.
- Added outdoor-use resilience: large targets, high contrast, audio transcripts, offline maps, rest amenities, weather awareness, and step-free rerouting.
- Reduced long historical text into a layered, audio-first story format.
- Made celebration optional and reflective rather than gamified.

## Visual system

- **Heritage teal:** `#075F5D`
- **Warm cream:** `#FBF7EF`
- **Tulip coral:** `#E96551`
- **Lake mist:** `#DCEFF0`
- **Dark ink:** `#173A3A`
- Elegant editorial serif for place and story titles, paired with a clear geometric sans serif for navigation and body content.
- Dutch architectural linework and tulip details are used sparingly to create local character without competing with usability.

01 / BRAND

A clinical system, not a clinical claim.

A cobalt identity, restrained typography and a consistent illustrated product geometry establish a recognizable system before individual channel briefs arrive.

Creative brief

Audience: skincare shoppers seeking a simpler routine. Objective: introduce a single hero serum, communicate its place in a routine, and direct customers to product education. The campaign trades exaggerated claims for a clear product name and an approachable ritual.

Fictional product, price, ingredients and benefits. No testing, consumer results, endorsements or commercial launch are claimed.

03 / EMAIL

One system, four lifecycle moments.

Live-text headlines, table-based layouts and a reusable visual structure distinguish teaser, launch, education and replenishment.

Unsent email specimens. Browser-preview SVGs must be replaced by hosted raster assets and tested in actual email clients before deployment.

04 / PAID

One message, nine placements.

Product-led and benefit-led variants isolate the hook; education and offer executions shift the message to different funnel stages.

Display production adaptations

Proposed test: compare product-first and benefit-first headlines in a matched placement, holding CTA, audience and image constant in a future controlled test. Evaluate CTR and conversion rate; no results have been measured.

Make room for moisture.

Motion prototype

Five-second CSS animation, a digital storyboard for a product reveal. No rendered video export.

05 / AMAZON

Answer the next shopping question.

A marketplace listing sequence separates product recognition from education, routine and size information.

Marketplace concept only. The primary SVG is an illustration, not an eligible photograph of a physical product. Confirm actual category and module requirements, product fill, raster format and listing rights before upload. Amazon image guidance.

06 / RETAIL

The campaign at shelf level.

Reduce the messaging to a distance-readable shelf headline and a useful associate education card.

Flat editable artwork, not printer-certified files. Allow 0.125 inch bleed when converting to print; confirm stock, trim, CMYK profile and printer proof.

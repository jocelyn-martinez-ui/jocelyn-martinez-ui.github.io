01 / BRAND SYSTEM

Clinical clarity.
Human warmth.

AI-generated photographic studies, not commercial photography or a tested product. Cropped and compressed for web delivery; campaign typography and layout are editable.

06 / MARKETPLACE

Make the next
question easy.

A sequence moves from product recognition to ingredient story, routine placement and size. The hierarchy is deliberately different from brand advertising.

Marketplace concept only. The primary image is a vector rendering, not a photograph of an actual product. Category rules, accepted image format and module specifications require verification before upload.

07 / RETAIL EDUCATION

On shelf.
In hand.

A short shelf headline attracts attention; a separate associate card holds the product and routine information that does not belong on the sign.

Flat vector artwork for review. Printer proofs, bleed, CMYK conversion and final legal copy are not complete.

# DERMÉA / Launch production notes

Independent fictional beauty campaign. Name is provisional and has not been trademark-cleared. No real client, sales, testing, endorsements or clinical data.

## Brief and brand standards
Audience: shoppers interested in uncomplicated hydration routines. Goal: communicate the serum's role and motivate exploration. Campaign proposition: Make room for moisture. Ink #10244B, cobalt #1748C6, ice #E7F1FA, paper #F6F5EF. Typeface: Arial/Helvetica system fallback. Keep pack geometry and label consistent. Use product-first visuals at high intent and education at lower intent.

## Illustrative production review
Initial simulated direction: one headline across every format. Proposed feedback: at leaderboard scale reduce to one line and a single CTA; reserve ingredient education for secondary marketplace modules. Implemented: narrow display artwork uses abbreviated messaging; retail card separates associate instructions from shelf headline. This is a self-directed design decision, not actual external feedback.

## Source and handoff
assets/*.svg are editable vector masters with live SVG text, gradient fills and viewBox dimensions. email/*.html are unsent table-based HTML specimens; corresponding .txt files supply subject, preview and copy. shop.html, style.css and app.js are responsive browser prototype sources. No Adobe native files, actual product photography, retouching or real campaign performance are included. The export studio can generate RGB raster proofs and browser-recorded vertical video in supported browsers; output is not pre-rendered or verified in all browsers. AI-assisted code and text were used; SVG artwork was programmatically drawn. No external image assets or custom fonts are required.

## Release checklist
- Meta: verify placement specs and safe zones, export PNG/JPG and check previews. https://eventsmanager.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image
- Google display: size studies are SVG masters, not accepted ad uploads; verify asset type, weight, placement and export. https://developers.google.com/publisher-tag/guides/ad-sizes
- Amazon: replace vector packshot with actual physical product photograph on pure white, verify frame fill and no added overlays; choose A+ module sizes in Seller Central. https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/G1881?language=en_US
- Email: replace SVG with hosted raster imagery and absolute URLs, add actual legal footer and unsubscribe functionality, test Outlook/Gmail/Apple Mail and dark mode. No sending.
- Print: confirm final dielines, bleed, CMYK conversion, fonts and regulatory copy with vendor. No press-ready claim.
- Testing: no actual advertising or commerce; define CTR, conversion rate, email click rate and retail comprehension as proposed measures. Compare headlines in matched placements before interpreting results.

## Second iteration
Eight lifecycle emails include four different layout structures in the expanded set: routine, cart, post-purchase and illustrative offer. Marketplace preview and retail shelf visualization embed editable vector artwork. Export studio provides original-dimension PNG/JPEG proof generation and a six-second canvas video recording, subject to browser support. No fictitious ratings or performance numbers.

## Editorial rebuild / third iteration
The visual system now uses ink #14283E, slate #68839C, ice #DCE5ED and warm paper #F5F3EE. Georgia and Arial are system typefaces. Three AI-generated photographic crops (hero, skin and gel) were transferred as verified, optimized WebP files. The hero crop has been cleaned of an invented registration mark and unsupported benefit copy. These are low-resolution web concept images, not licensed stock or photographs of a real product; use higher-resolution consistently branded assets for final commercial delivery. The vector packshot and generated hero are separate explorations, not a guaranteed identical bottle. The campaign, four primary ads, five display formats, four marketplace secondary images, A+ module, label, two retail print layouts and all eight emails were recomposed. The web case study now prioritizes a visual opening, a smaller number of showcased specimens, and links to source files. Email specimens use WebP previews; actual email client compatibility and production exports remain unverified. No native Adobe files or fabricated performance figures.

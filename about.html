ABOUT JOCELYN

Clarity in the interaction.
Character in the details.

I’m a Visual & UI Designer with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from Hope College. I connect visual identity, information and interaction—and use code to make the behavior inspectable.

The way I think

I like making complicated situations easier to understand: what a record means, what happens after a button is pressed, and how someone recovers when things do not go as expected.

Visual design is part of that work. Typography, color and composition should help a person orient themselves while giving the product a distinct point of view.

The technical foundation

My Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from Hope College gives me a practical way to think about state, data and implementation. I use HTML, CSS and JavaScript to turn design decisions into working prototypes.

I want that knowledge to make collaboration with engineers more precise: clearer component behavior, more realistic constraints and fewer unanswered questions between a screen and a product.

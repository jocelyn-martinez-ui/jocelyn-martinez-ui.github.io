Design decision: The prior archive piece presented simulated performance scores and a photographed bottle that did not establish the product identity. This revision replaces both with a coherent original still life and an explicit three-stage reading sequence.

Product, performance, consumer votes and note profile are fictional concept material. Artwork is inline SVG; headings and note descriptions remain real selectable text. The stage buttons update one panel without relying on color alone.

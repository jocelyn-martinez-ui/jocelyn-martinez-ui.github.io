Vector objects use **approximate CMYK percentages**, but embedded concept photography is RGB and is **not** converted using a printer-approved ICC profile. No PDF/X conformity, physical bleed proof, trapping/overprint test, ink limit, image effective-resolution sign-off or printer-certified proof is claimed.

**Packaging caveats**: BRUME is a hypothetical electrical table-light product. Source does not contain verified electrical ratings, power specifications, warnings, regulatory symbols, safety certification, disposal statements, actual measurements, barcodes or retailer terms. NEVER use the specimen as a product label or safety instruction in commercial distribution without qualified manufacturer review.

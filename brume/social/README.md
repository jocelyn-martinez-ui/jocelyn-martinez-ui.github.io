# BRUME campaign asset manifest

15 designed SVG source layouts with separately editable vector text and linked AI-generated concept photography (5 feed-style images, 6 carousel chapters, 3 vertical stories and 1 landscape). Flattened PNG exports of the same layouts included in the downloadable project. Each source links `../images/<photo>.jpg` and must retain image file structure.

The photographs are generated concept images of imaginary glass lamps; they do not show any fabricated or manufactured product. No Pinterest photography is copied or republished.

Platform formats: feed/carousel 1080×1350; story 1080×1920; wide composition 1200×627. Platform safe zones and advertising formats should be verified against current requirements before publication. The images are design samples, not published advertising.

# PROOF / Creative direction and researched reference notes

**Project date:** 24 October 2026. **Scope:** fictional creative-production laboratory and three self-directed, AI-assisted design commissions. Research references guide principles only; none of their artwork is copied.

## Why the old PROOF was not enough
The previous version gave strong evidence of explicit revision approval logic, stable state, and preflight gating, but only three fairly repetitive display-type posters and an interface dominated by operational UI. It under-demonstrated adaptable visual art direction, in-depth editorial layouts, marketing assets and actual source master variety.

## Portfolio-wide contrast
- AFTERFORM: soft, emotive fashion-repair visual language and garment tags. **Avoid clothing, repair-as-memory, plum-lilac fashion editorial.**
- INTERVAL: event art direction, typographic concert identity. **Avoid event-poster-only identity.**
- BRUME: photographic sculptural domestic product. **Avoid staged product photography and lifestyle look.**
- Áurea / Novella: content browsing, reading interfaces. **Avoid making this a consumer app case.**
- ImHere / Holland: practical attendance and civic information UX. **Avoid another end-user flow-led case.**
- Earlier PROOF: blue + citron, compressed headline, cards and approval UI. **Avoid a SaaS-first, monochrome typographic look.**

## Three visual explorations evaluated
1. **Riso print lab:** two-color layers, registration, density, granular pattern, expressive type. High marketing/editorial originality; risks pseudo-authentic print claims. Selected for PULP only.
2. **Quiet field publication:** slow reading, tactile abstract study, old-paper warm neutrals, serif and restrained botanical/architectural lines. Distinct from PULP; selected for FIELD.
3. **Specimen manufacturer:** detailed material rendering, muted finish swatches, strict codes, repeatable technical label grids. Less expressive but strongest in-house production role fit; selected for TERRA.

**Design decision:** three visibly distinct commissions within a calm PROOF studio shell. One common design identity would erase the range the portfolio needs.

## The work
### Commission A / PULP
Fictional ink-for-makers brand. Original campaign slogan **COLOUR IS MATTER**. Three editable posters, three feed masters and one story. Expressive two-layer vector textures and editorial typography. **No live brand client, social results or claimed risograph separation.**

### Commission B / FIELD
Fictional 12-page editorial artifact examining material traces and the form of print. A5 page-by-page original text and editable abstract graphics. Intended to demonstrate running heads, folios, typography, image density and sequencing. **No actual binding or printer proof.**

### Commission C / TERRA
Fictional B2B architectural ceramic finish samples: Mist, Ember, Chalk. Three source label fronts, A5 ad, A4 sell sheet and two archived revisions; planned 3 mm bleed. **No real electrical/structural/material performance claims, vendor dieline or ICC proof.**

# PROOF — Visual Coherence Revision

**Revision date:** 24 October 2026. This is a source-artwork coherence pass, not a new project, claim of commercial delivery, or physical production report.

## What was wrong
The original 38 SVGs used recurring abstract marks without consistently articulating the products, editorial story or materials:
- **PULP:** similar random gestures and backgrounds across every poster; the twelve-page guide was four repeated abstract motifs rather than usable standards.
- **FIELD:** multiple pages reused the same circle, squiggle and grid illustrations irrespective of editorial subject.
- **TERRA:** plain gradients and crossed lines failed to suggest a consistent material finish; the campaign ad and sell sheet seemed disconnected from label artwork.

## What changed
### Commission 01 — PULP / Ink collection
Seven campaign images now share an expressive but *repeatable* visual grammar: ink-spill silhouettes, halftone register texture, a consistent PULP wordmark, an explicit fictional ink-can packaging illustration, real image hierarchy, and a controlled color palette.

**Digital palette:** deep teal `#203F3D`, coral `#C85243`, blush `#E6ABA4`, golden ink `#F2C65A`, ocean teal `#43807B`, uncoated-paper `#F5EBDA`.

Three posters address the brand promise, overprinting and product collection. The four social variations communicate launch, process, packaging and a custom vertical story. They do not repeat identical layouts.

**Twelve-page brand guide** was rewritten to provide purposeful specifications and illustrations: positioning, mark clear space, typography, color roles, overlapping imagery, graphic devices, poster anatomy, social adaptation, email design, correct/incorrect use, source-file nomenclature.

### Commission 02 — FIELD / Material journal
Twelve A5 pages now follow one quiet botanical/archival identity, consistent page furniture and a real reading sequence: cover, editor's note, contents, three chapter markers, cross-section study, botanical specimen, pressure impressions, interval study, archive, colophon.

**Palette:** deep forest `#293D33`, paper `#F1EDE2`, sage `#879F88`, terracotta `#AB765C`, sand `#CEBFA8`.

The imagery is purposely illustration-based and represents an original *conceptual field journal*, not photographs, actual specimens or observed scientific results. The sources remain individual A5 pages, not imposed press signatures.

### Commission 03 — TERRA / Architectural sample system
Three specimen labels plus two archived versions use consistent brand and product-data zones, newly illustrated material surfaces with a shared texture treatment, and edition-specific finish colors. A5 advertising and A4 sales sheet reproduce the **same finish system** instead of introducing unrelated abstract graphics.

**Sample finishes:** Mist, Ember, Chalk. R01/R02 remain deliberately outdated fictional states and R03 remains concept-current (not approved).

Dimensions remain:
- Label source 96 × 136 mm, planned trim 90 × 130 mm, provisional 3 mm all sides.
- A5 ad source 154 × 216 mm, intended trim 148 × 210 mm.
- A4 sell sheet source 216 × 303 mm, intended trim 210 × 297 mm.

All sources are RGB editable SVG. Print profiles, color separations, licensing, substrate claims, supplier dielines and physical proofs are not verified.

## Integration
- Case-study gallery sources retain their paths, so no broken links.
- Homepage, graphic collection and case-study image references include a new version key to avoid the old graphics staying in caches.
- Dynamic review imagery uses the same version key.
- Relative preflight CSS guides are corrected to the actual physical dimensions rather than approximate equal margins.

## Checks performed
- All 38 SVG source files passed opening/closing SVG tag-balance checks and include titles.
- All four print-output group types retain explicit physical dimensions.
- All 38 are independent vector files, without cross-origin JPEG references.
- Full browser screenshot review, color-managed soft proof, font licensing check and real printer verification are still required.

## Scope boundaries
No original user-supplied photography or licensed Pinterest references were copied into the artwork. An image-generated creative reference was used to assess cohesion, while the committed project deliverables remain original, editable SVG sources. Do not represent the concept materials, client-like feedback, products or production tests as real commercial work.

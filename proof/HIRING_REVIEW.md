# PROOF / Graphic Design Hiring Review — October 2026

This is a qualitative hiring-oriented audit of a **fictional, AI-assisted portfolio project**. It is not an employer's judgment or a verified competency certificate.

## Reviewer A / Creative director
**Evidence strongest:** PULP's seven materially distinct poster/social compositions, a 12-page identity-guide system, and visible creative rationale. Three separate briefs make the studio's visual flexibility clearer than the old PROOF identity alone.

**Concern:** the riso appearance is designed in SVG; it has not been printed, photographed, color-separated or art directed on a physical press. Work could improve with actual process photography and strong commissioned briefs.

**Interview positioning:** explain why image structure, halftone density and hierarchy shift between poster and vertical mobile social artwork.

## Reviewer B / In-house graphic design manager
**Evidence strongest:** distinct brand voice by client-like brief; three responsive marketing email mock implementations; a full publication sequence; and version-controlled production artwork.

**Concern:** actual email client tests, deadline-driven multi-stakeholder collaboration, paid campaign performance and Photoshop/InDesign/Illustrator native file workflow are not demonstrated by generated SVG/HTML sources.

**Interview positioning:** emphasize the actual files, review decisions and ability to keep masters coherent; never imply that fictional campaigns were real work.

## Reviewer C / Print production manager
**Evidence strongest:** dimensioned TERRA label family and print advertising files, explicit revisions, archived inaccurate states and correct distinction between approval logic and vendor sign-off.

**Concern:** A5 FIELD pages have no commercial bleed; PULP poster source lacks real print dimensions; the website's preflight graphics are illustrative only. The label sources are RGB SVG, not vendor ICC-managed CMYK or PDF/X, and no physical prints have been measured. Actual SKU/product-data and legal compliance require supplier validation.

**Interview positioning:** demonstrate which checks are technical vs hypothetical and articulate the real proofing process.

## Recommended next upgrades

2. Convert an editorial issue to press-imposed, bleed-aware PDF/X under a printer's supplied specs.
3. Test email templates in real Outlook, Gmail and Apple Mail and document screenshots, discrepancies and fixes.
4. Demonstrate native Adobe authoring with source screenshots and legitimate licensed fonts.
5. Replace one fictional commission with a real paid or volunteer brief if possible.

**Conclusion:** the redesign adds meaningful breadth for graphic design screening while honestly remaining conceptual. Graphic quality and production readiness should be independently inspected before using it as paid commercial evidence.

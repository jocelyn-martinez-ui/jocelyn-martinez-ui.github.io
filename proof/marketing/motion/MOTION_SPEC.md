# Motion prototypes / real browser interactions

Two real, autoplaying 8-second CSS motion explorations: (1) image-to-type and (2) revision emphasis. Both have keyboard-operable pause/play controls, reduced-motion static fallback and original campaign imagery. They are real browser motion prototypes but **not** MP4/animated GIF exports or social-platform-tested deliverables. Before sending to a brand, export at approved frame rates, resolutions, codecs and durations, ensure video-specific accessibility and check platform file limits.

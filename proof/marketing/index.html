01 / THE BRIEFINTEGRATED EDITORIAL CAMPAIGN

Different formats.
One point of view.

Promote an original design publication while demonstrating the craft behind the finished file.

PROOF is a fictional design-publishing concept. Its audience hypothesis is people interested in design decisions, image-making, visual craft and print. This campaign uses original source imagery from the PROOF project; no real audience research, ad results, open rates or mailing list are claimed.

The creative solution pairs monochrome contact-sheet imagery with high-signal orange, acid yellow and controlled typographic rhythm. The aesthetic differs from BRUME's domestic product photography and the earlier, softer editorial portfolio projects.

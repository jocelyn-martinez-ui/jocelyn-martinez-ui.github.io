Subject: PROOF / One idea, five ways of seeing it
Preheader: How a single campaign adapts from social feed to story, email and print.

CONSISTENT. NOT IDENTICAL.
A good visual identity doesn't mean repeating one layout. It means making new decisions from the same set of principles.

SOCIAL — Lead with an image and a fast message.
EMAIL — Use live text, readable rhythm and a clear CTA.
PRINT — Change scale and account for physical output.

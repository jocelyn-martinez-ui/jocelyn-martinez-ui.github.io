ABOUT / AN INDEPENDENT STUDIO CONCEPT

LOOK
AGAIN.

PLAIN SIGHT is a self-initiated creative identity exploring the relationship between photography, design and attention. It is not represented as a registered agency with clients or employees.

THE POINT OF VIEW

Ordinary, by
design.

A design exercise in creating a coherent photographic language, not a testimonial or client campaign.

WHY THIS CONCEPT EXISTS

Attention
is a medium.

Good editorial art direction is not the addition of more design. It is knowing when an image can carry the page, when text should interrupt, and when whitespace should remain empty.

The project uses three themes—human gestures, composed objects and quiet architecture—to demonstrate visual continuity without repeating one subject or one layout.

Created by Jocelyn Martinez as a self-directed portfolio concept. Five original images were generated with AI and shaped into consistent editorial compositions, publication spreads and campaign files. No commissioned photographer, models, agency clients or real physical production are implied.

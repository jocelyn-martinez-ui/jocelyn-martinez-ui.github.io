ARRANGEMENTS

Objects can keep a conversation.

One of three visual studies developed for the PLAIN SIGHT identity. Imagery and narratives are self-directed.

STUDY 02PHOTOGRAPHIC ART DIRECTION / GENERATED IMAGE STUDY2026

01 / THE PREMISE

Learn to
notice.

A flower leans, a glass holds light, a stone anchors the edge of the frame. Arrangement is a way of asking ordinary things to speak.

Glass, linen and botanical forms explore texture, weight and asymmetric balance. From wider arrangements to closely observed detail, the study is about how objects become visual punctuation.

02 / THE EDIT

The edit
is the idea.

These crops are not presented as separate original photographs. Their purpose is to show how negative space, scale and photographic sequence affect the reading of the same source material.

Source disclosure: Two AI-generated still-life compositions and four editorial crop variants. Not commissioned product photography.

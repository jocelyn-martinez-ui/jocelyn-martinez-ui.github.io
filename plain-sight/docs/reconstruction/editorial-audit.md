# Editorial and campaign reconstruction

The previous 24-page PDF is preserved at `downloads/archive/PLAIN_SIGHT_Studies_Edition02.pdf`. Original rendered contact sheets in this folder record all pages before changes. The new canonical file is `downloads/PLAIN_SIGHT_Studies_01-03.pdf`; its 24 exact previews and extracted text are generated from that file, not separately composed website substitutes.

This is a digital review edition, not a commercially preflighted or physically proofed book. Generated scenes remain transparently identified. Page numbering describes actual PDF pages, not fictitious spreads.

## Render checks and corrections

All 24 pages were rendered with Poppler and visually reviewed in a contact sheet. Inspection prompted wrapped crop headings on pages 7 and 13, more space between title and imagery on page 21, and less immediate repetition on chapter openers 5 and 17. Their reader descriptions were then corrected to match the actual images. The final PDF has 24 A4 portrait pages and embedded type. The exact page images, text companion and PDF are generated together.

The supplied sources are screen-scale images. Keeping complete compositions and embedding optimized JPEGs reduced the first reconstruction export from approximately 18MB to approximately 3.1MB. That comparison concerns two reconstruction exports, **not** a measured improvement over the old publication or website loading.

## Campaign review

All six posters and six square social artworks were visually inventoried in the before contact sheets. All 35 SVG assets were structurally audited. Four masters received targeted repairs; their previous SVGs are preserved in `campaign-before/`:

- Poster 02: move the headline off the dark jacket, retain the full seated composition below it, and establish three readable headline lines.
- Social 01 and 04: shorten the image region and separate the title from the footer.
- Social 05: restore the complete “IN BETWEEN” name and split the small footer into its own column-safe lines.

Other campaign artwork is retained. The site exposes all six posters and all six square social pieces on mobile, where the previous stylesheet hid selected artwork. The historical Georgia/Arial SVG font choices, banners, stories, carousel masters and eight-page identity guide have not all been rebuilt into the new web typography. The existing identity-guide PDF is labelled as the previous edition on the website; this system document defines the reconstruction. A physical proof and broader campaign-font conversion remain future production work.

## Strongest remaining editorial question

Five originals can support 24 pages when the book is explicitly about framing. Repetition is therefore purposeful but still a constraint. A future edition should add genuinely different source compositions only if a new editorial question requires them. More near-duplicate scenes would weaken the premise. Reading pace and annotation usefulness need external critique; this reconstruction does not claim tested reader preference.

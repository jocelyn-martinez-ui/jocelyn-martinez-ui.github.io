# PLAIN SIGHT — Art-Direction Decisions

## Where the photographic language begins

Rather than positioning a fictional physical product as the hero, PLAIN SIGHT makes **looking itself** the subject. A single photographic grammar links three otherwise unrelated subjects: human presence, tactile objects and architectural silence.

### GESTURES / PEOPLE

- Tone: intimate but not sentimental.
- Lighting: low-angle window illumination and fabric highlights.
- Framing: full-room portrait followed by a close crop of drapery or posture.
- Tension: an ordinary action becomes more expressive when isolated and sequenced.
- Original generated images: `gesture-seated.webp`, `gesture-fabric.webp`.
- Edited crops: `gesture-seated-detail.webp`, `gesture-seated-space.webp`, `gesture-fabric-detail.webp`, `gesture-fabric-fabric.webp`.

### ARRANGEMENTS / OBJECTS

- Tone: sculptural and earthy, not generic product advertising.
- Material system: veined floral surfaces, transparent water, matte plaster, linen and stone.
- Lighting: single warm directional source with restrained shadow gradients.
- Sequence: single-stem simplicity followed by a denser table composition.
- Original generated images: `arrangement-bloom.webp`, `arrangement-table.webp`.
- The remaining arrangement images are detail studies/crops of these original sources.

### IN BETWEEN / SPACES

- Tone: contemplative, warm, inhabited only by evidence of previous presence.
- Geometry: deep opening, single chair, triangular band of sunlight, a niche and stone floor.
- Sequence: whole room, window detail, chair and moving shadow.
- Original generated image: `between-room.webp`.
- All the additional frames in this chapter are crops of one room illustration, not images of several spaces.

## Editorial principles

1. Give the subject permission to occupy an entire frame.
2. Use 10–20% of the pages for typographic or whitespace-led pauses.
3. Use image contrast and proportion rather than arbitrary overlays.
4. Keep photographer-style credits transparent. Since all original photographs were AI-generated illustrations, do not imply a real photographer or studio shoot.
5. Present alternate crops honestly as alternate crops.
6. Change poster composition intentionally: full-image overlay, horizontal band, hard split, image window and purely typographic variant.
7. Keep type editable in SVG. Embed the WebP photographic material for portability; image content remains raster.

## Storytelling and hiring presentation

Best portfolio opening order: (1) website and art direction, (2) five original photographic sources, (3) publication pages, (4) posters, (5) social/email/stationery and (6) system details. Explain the limits without apologizing for the work: concept, generated illustration and observable deliverables rather than fabricated client outcomes.

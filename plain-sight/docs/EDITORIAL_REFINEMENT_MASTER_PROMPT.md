## Audit Edition 01 critically
Edition 01 uses expressive serif type but repeats that same voice at almost every scale; project cards and 24-page chapter layouts recur too predictably. Its five master photographs generate many derivative crops, weakening variety when those crops are treated as new frames. Refine the type and positioning first. Use cropping only where it changes interpretation. Restrain decorations, but do not confuse restraint with blankness or lack of conceptual decisions.

## Four typography roles — specify hierarchy precisely
1. **Editorial display:** modern high-contrast Bodoni Moda variable or licensed equivalent. Optical serif hierarchy, not condensed faux-luxury text; use for decisive statements. Desktop 70–260px depending on proportion, mobile 60–115px.
2. **Expressive accent:** Instrument Serif italic or deliberate equivalent; reserve for short emotional transitions, never for everything. Let italic alter meaning instead of decorating a heading.
3. **Functional reading:** DM Sans or Inter; 15–18px desktop, 14–16px phone; 1.6–1.75 line height, moderate paragraph width, readable contrast and sufficient breaks.
4. **Catalog metadata:** IBM Plex Mono, small uppercase or sentence case; image numbers, labels, folios, chapter IDs, print specifications and credits. Navigation remains comfortable to tap; decorative metadata may be finer than functional labels.
Specify true typography: font family, optical size, contrast, tracking, line spacing, baseline, hanging punctuation, rag, start and end alignment. Don’t use only one formula for every heading.

## Grid and placement rules
Adopt a 12-column desktop system with 16–30px gutters and responsive 2-column/tablet, 1-column/mobile reading flows. Page gutters `clamp(22px,5.25vw,104px)`. Baseline: 8px where possible. Enforce anchored edges: chapter meta, titles, body, image frame, caption and navigation each intentionally align. Each composition needs a dominant element, a supporting element and a controlled pause. Split photographic sequences in proportions such as 5/3/4 columns, not three identical image cards. A type/image collision must earn its existence and never obscure faces or controls. Provide sufficient safe margins and prevent x-overflow at 390px.

Palette: paper `#F2EFE9`, ivory `#FAF8F2`, ink `#292724`, stroke `#B9B2A8`, oxblood `#713F44`, dark-warm `#40373A`. Use photography for richer tonal variation; do not add unmotivated gradients, glassmorphism, floating product cards or generic smooth motion.

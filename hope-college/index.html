Designing for participation.

Make campus opportunities easy to discover, understand and act on. The work brings together student involvement, cultural exchange, academic inquiry and community service.

Students scanning a busy bulletin board, opening an email between classes or browsing campus resources need a clear message, meaningful context and an accessible next step.

07 / Digital assets

One idea. Many formats.

Layouts change with the format: a wide hero, square announcement, compact newsletter header and distance-readable signage.

08 / Integrated campaign

A coherent student experience.

The involvement fair becomes a recognizable message across a campus noticeboard, a student guide, a website and an email reminder.

09 / Production

From layout to delivery.

Print

Trim-sized vector originals and RGB layout proofs with 0.125-inch bleed and trim boxes. Equal-panel Z-fold brochure; confirm folds and stock with the printer. CMYK conversion, image resolution and press proofing remain required.

Email

600 px maximum-width layout, responsive mobile padding, live text, alt text and plain-text companion files. Tested in a browser at 375 and 600 px; real inbox compatibility and sender links require final testing.

Digital

Six size-specific RGB compositions with editable vector masters and PNG exports. Website artwork should be paired with live HTML text and accessible calls to action in production.

Brand references: Hope College brand identity · Color palette · Typography.

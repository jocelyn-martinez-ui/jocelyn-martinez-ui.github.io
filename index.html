01 / About

Design shaped by empathy, structure, and technical curiosity.

I care about how a product feels, but I also care about whether people understand it, trust it, recover from mistakes, and can realistically use it.

My computer science background helps me understand constraints and systems. My design practice keeps those systems accountable to the people inside them.

I work across UX strategy, interaction design, visual design, prototyping, and front-end implementation. I am especially drawn to products that need warmth without losing clarity—education, culture, wellness, public services, and thoughtfully designed consumer experiences.

I approach each project by clarifying the real task, separating assumptions from evidence, and giving complex information a readable structure.

01

Frame the product

Define users, constraints, roles, system boundaries, and the decisions the interface must support.

02

Shape the interaction

Build flows, hierarchy, feedback, permissions, empty states, and recovery paths—not only ideal screens.

03

Craft the interface

Create responsive visual systems with intentional typography, accessible interaction, and implementation awareness.

03 / Approach

A process that keeps beautiful ideas accountable.

I do not treat UX terminology as evidence. I connect each design decision to a user task, system condition, observed pattern, or clearly labeled hypothesis.

01

Clarify

Define the user, task, context, constraints, stakeholders, and what success must actually mean.

02

Structure

Map information, roles, flows, permissions, feedback, and failure states before polishing screens.

03

Craft

Build a visual system that supports hierarchy, accessibility, responsive behavior, and product character.

04

Validate

Separate assumptions from findings, test the risky moments, and revise the product—not only the presentation.

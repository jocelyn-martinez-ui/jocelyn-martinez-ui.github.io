# NODO / Print source masters and simulated revisions

**Artwork deliverables:** Three label front designs, plus an A5 advertisement, A6 landscape promotional postcard, A6 instruction insert, A4 product sell sheet and two archived CLIP label revisions. **Nine editable SVG masters total.**

Every SVG has a physical document size with **provisional 3 mm artwork bleed**, e.g. `label-clip-r03.svg` is 96 × 136 mm for a planned 90 × 130 mm trim. Printer specifications remain unverified and the design is not supplier-certified or retail-compliant. Digital RGB SVG colors are not print CMYK separations.

## Revision example / CLIP

- **R01:** generic “CABLE HOLDER”, 04 units, preliminary content.
- **R02:** clarified “CABLE CLIP”, still 06 SKU not synchronized to package quantity — a deliberately detectable mismatch.
- **R03:** “Desk-cable landing clips”, QTY 06, aligned to sample SKU. The revision highlights the need for content QA, not only visual QA.

All revision requests are fictional teaching examples; the mismatch and its correction did not come from a client, regulatory reviewer, or printer. R03 is conceptually current, not commercially approved.

## Production checklist

- Verify the vendor's exact dieline, stock, label adhesive, usable print area, barcode, lot code, required legal copy, and product measurements.
- Confirm quantity, SKU, all marketing claims, contact data, and final product photography.
- Select printer ICC profile and color mapping; proof all paper/media effects and spot plates.
- Inspect font embedding/outline policy, TrimBox/BleedBox, CMYK separations, overprint, trapping, QR/barcode scan at actual size, and vendor PDF/X settings.
- Obtain a real press proof and physical sample prior to commercial production.

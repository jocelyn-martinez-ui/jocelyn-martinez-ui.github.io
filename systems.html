StateControl behaviorFeedback and recovery
ReadyInput remains editable.The helper text explains what saving means.
InvalidNo sample value is committed.Focus returns to the input; a connected error explains what to fix.
SavingSubmission is temporarily disabled.Visible “Saving…” feedback; a short local demonstration delay.
SavedA receipt replaces the form.The saved text is escaped, announced and reversible with Undo.
UnavailableThe action is natively disabled.A visible explanation provides the reason.

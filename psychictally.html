01 / THE DESIGN PROBLEM

Not every story fits a star rating.

Firsthand experiences and predictions often change meaning as time passes. A useful community needs more than attractive discussion cards: readers need context, contributors need boundaries, and moderators need transparent workflows.

The product separates conversations, reviews, reader-directory entries and follow-ups instead of flattening everything into one feed. This is a design interpretation informed by the existing codebase, not the outcome of a completed user study.

07 / REFLECTION & NEXT VALIDATION

What I'd test
before claiming success.

Does a new visitor understand the difference between reviews and forum discussions? Can someone find a saved discussion again? Can a moderator predict what a report action will change?

I would use task-based observation and keyboard/mobile walkthroughs, then document findings without converting assumptions into invented statistics. The research plan is written; the study is not represented as completed.

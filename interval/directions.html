Pressure & release

A loud invitation. A calm programme. A deliberate gap between blocks becomes the identity’s recurring interval.

Typography: Unbounded display + Instrument Sans utility.

Fit: The visual energy belongs to the announcement; clear rows, time labels and restrained motion belong to the planning task. It adds a graphic voice absent from the current portfolio.

Watch: Limit chartreuse to key actions and identity panels. Wide type stays out of long text.

Resonant matter

Copper, paper and close material detail. A quieter, collected identity that foregrounds the physical sources of sound.

Typography: Editorial serif + pragmatic grotesk.

Fit: Rich printed matter and object photography.

Why not lead: Warm editorial territory is already represented by Áurea and Novella. It risks softening the festival’s public invitation.

INTERVALAN OPEN SCORE

Night score

Cobalt, negative space and a compressed programme grid. Strong spatial signage and nocturnal campaign potential.

Typography: Condensed headlines + monospace programme labels.

Fit: Evening performance communication.

Why not lead: It overemphasizes nightlife and suggests a louder event. A daytime, seated listening experience needs a broader emotional range.
